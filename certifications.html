Our accreditations demonstrate an ongoing commitment to quality, safety and environmental responsibility — and give clients confidence that every job is delivered to recognised standards.

All gas works are carried out by Gas Safe registered engineers — the legal requirement for anyone working on gas appliances in the UK.

NICEIC

Our electrical services are delivered by NICEIC accredited engineers, ensuring safe, certified installations and testing.

CHAS

CHAS accreditation confirms our health and safety processes meet recognised industry standards.

Constructionline Gold

Constructionline Gold membership demonstrates enhanced pre-qualification across governance, health and safety, and financial standing.

ISO 9001

Certified quality management — consistent processes and continual improvement across all our services.

ISO 14001

Certified environmental management — reducing our impact and supporting clients’ sustainability goals.

ISO 45001

Certified occupational health and safety management — a safe working environment for our teams and clients on every project.
