Cleaning & Grounds Maintenance Staff

Maintain estates with daily cleaning, waste removal and high-standard grounds upkeep.

The Role

Maintain estates with daily cleaning, waste removal and high-standard grounds upkeep. You will join a team guided by our values of Community, Accountability, Trust and Operations, working across housing, public sector, commercial and hospitality environments.

What You’ll Do

  • Deliver daily and scheduled cleaning across estates and premises
  • Carry out waste removal and specialist deep cleans
  • Maintain grounds and outdoor spaces to a high standard
  • Report issues and hazards promptly

What We Look For

  • Reliability and pride in high standards
  • Experience in cleaning or grounds work helpful, not essential
  • A friendly, professional manner with residents and clients
  • Willingness to learn and develop

What We Offer

  • Ongoing training, apprenticeships and career development
  • A safe workplace — ISO 45001 accredited
  • An inclusive culture where everyone is valued
  • Work that makes a real difference in homes and communities
