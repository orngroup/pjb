Damp & Mould Remediation Teams

Diagnose issues, apply treatments and restore properties to safe living conditions.

The Role

Diagnose issues, apply treatments and restore properties to safe living conditions. You will join a team guided by our values of Community, Accountability, Trust and Operations, working across housing, public sector, commercial and hospitality environments.

What You’ll Do

  • Survey and diagnose damp, mould and condensation issues
  • Apply treatments and complete remediation works to statutory timescales
  • Restore affected areas including plastering, decoration and ventilation improvements
  • Record evidence to support Awaab’s Law compliance reporting

What We Look For

  • Experience in damp/mould remediation or related trades
  • Understanding of Awaab’s Law obligations desirable
  • A respectful, resident-focused manner
  • Attention to detail in recording works

What We Offer

  • Ongoing training, apprenticeships and career development
  • A safe workplace — ISO 45001 accredited
  • An inclusive culture where everyone is valued
  • Work that makes a real difference in homes and communities
